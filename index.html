AISystemicRisk.com

Descriptive .com domain for the concept of “AI systemic risk”: the risk that AI models, infrastructures and usage patterns may create, amplify or transmit shocks across financial markets, critical infrastructures and the wider economy.

Intended for central banks, supervisors, financial-stability boards, systemic banking & insurance groups, global reinsurers, market infrastructures, risktech / datatech providers and think tanks that need a clear, long-lived label for AI systemic risk — separate from vendor brands and product names.

Any frameworks, indices, dashboards or observatories built under this banner would be fully designed and operated by the future acquirer.
